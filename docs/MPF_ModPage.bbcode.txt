[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- FEATURES[/u][/b][/size][/color]
[list]
[*][size=2]Applies face morphs to NPCs at runtime, paired (or not) with OBody presets - without baking FaceGen![/size][/*]
[*][size=2]Works over both vanilla NPCs and NPC overhauls.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2]Supports multiple INI files so content mods can remain independent.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2]No per-NPC OBody JSON needed - the physique names the presets.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2]Children are refused by the plugin, whatever the list says.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2]Ships no morphs and no NPC list: on its own it changes nothing in your game.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2]Open-source SKSE plugin.[/size][/*]
[/list]

[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- FOR MOD AUTHORS[/u][/b][/size][/color]

[b]How to build:[/b]

[b]1)[/b] Create a .tri file with your face morphs and place it in
[code]Data\SKSE\Plugins\MPF\morphs\[/code]

[b][color=#b45f06]Give it a unique filename![/color][/b] Every content mod shares this one folder, so a generic name like [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]FACE_FemaleHead.tri[/font][/size] will be overwritten by the next mod that ships one. It is also good practice to prefix it with something of yours: [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]MYMOD_FemaleHead.tri[/font][/size].


[b]2)[/b] Create your own [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace].toml[/font][/size] manifest in that same folder and declare your .tri:

[code][[sets]]
file = "MYMOD_FemaleHead.tri"
sex  = "female"          ; male | female
part = "face"            ; misc | face | eyes | hair | facialhair | scar | eyebrows[/code]

[b][color=#b45f06]Name it after your mod[/color][/b] - [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]MYMOD_morphs.toml[/font][/size], not [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]morphs.toml[/font][/size]. MPF reads every .toml in that folder, so yours sits beside everybody else's instead of replacing it. Copy [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]Docs\morphs.toml.example[/font][/size] to start from a working file.

[b]part[/b] says which kind of head part the TRI is for - [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]face[/font][/size] for a head, [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]eyebrows[/font][/size] for brows, [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]facialhair[/font][/size] for beards, and so on. These are the game's own head part types.


[b]3)[/b] Create a .ini file in [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]Data\ModernPhysiqueFramework\[/font][/size] following the structure below.

Name it after your mod, MPF reads every .ini in that folder, so you never edit anybody else's file:

[code][Settings]
Priority = 200

[Physique.<NAME YOU PICKED FOR YOUR PHYSIQUE>]
FaceMorph     = <the morph name inside your .tri>
MalePresets   = Preset 01 | Preset 02
FemalePresets = Preset 01 | Preset 02

[Physique.Heavy]
FaceMorph     = RLY_Heavy
MalePresets   = MPP_Heavy
FemalePresets = MPP_Heavy

[NPCs]
Name/ID = Weight, Physique
Alvor   = 75, Heavy
0x0001347F~Skyrim.esm = auto, Heavy

[Factions]
Faction name  = Weight, Physique, Distribution chance
BanditFaction = 100, Heavy, 30%[/code]

[b]- FaceMorph[/b] is the morph name [i]inside[/i] the .tri file, not a filename and not a BodySlide slider (although usually it is).
[b]
- MalePresets[/b] / [b]FemalePresets[/b] are OBody preset names and are optional - without them MPF still applies the face morph and skips OBody safely.

- In [b][NPCs][/b] the value after the comma is the [b]physique[/b] - the name of a [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace][Physique.<name>][/font][/size] section - not a preset. Names are EditorIDs (they need po3's Tweaks); a FormID like [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]0x0001347F~Skyrim.esm [/font][/size]works with nothing extra.

[b]- Priority[/b] decides who wins when two files name the same NPC or physique: higher wins, and a tie is broken by filename, so the result never depends on load order. Physiques of Skyrim ships at 100 - go above it to override it without touching its files. Every conflict is written to the log.


[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- WEIGHT IS PHYSIQUE STRENGTH, NOT FAT[/u][/b][/size][/color]

[b]The single thing authors get wrong.[/b]

The number in [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace][NPCs][/font][/size] is [b]not[/b] the vanilla fat slider. It is how strongly that physique is applied, from 0 (nothing) to 100 (the full morph).

[code]Narfi = 95, Thin      ; 95% of the Thin morph - very thin
Narfi = 40, Thin      ; 40% of the Thin morph - only slightly thin[/code]

Writing a low number for a thin NPC gives you the opposite of what you meant: a low weight means [i]barely morphed[/i], not [i]very thin[/i].

The same number scales the face morph, so the face and the body always agree - that is what keeps a heavy face on a heavy body without anyone authoring a second number. Use [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]auto[/font][/size] to read the NPC's own record weight instead.


[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- FOLDER STRUCTURE[/u][/b][/size][/color]

In this page you'll install these files:

[code]Data\
├── SKSE\Plugins\
│   ├── MPF.dll                                the plugin
│   └── MPF\
│       └── morphs\                            (empty - content mods fill it)
├── ModernPhysiqueFramework\                   (empty - content mods fill it)
└── Docs\
    ├── ModernPhysiqueFramework.schema.ini     the INI format, fully commented
    └── morphs.toml.example                    copy this to start your manifest[/code]

[b]And your mod should look like this:[/b]

[code]MyPhysiqueMod\
├── SKSE\
│   └── Plugins\
│       └── MPF\
│           └── morphs\
│               ├── MYMOD_FemaleHead.tri       your morphs - use a unique filename!
│               ├── MYMOD_MaleHead.tri
│               └── MYMOD_morphs.toml          your manifest - name it after your mod
└── ModernPhysiqueFramework\
    └── MyPhysiqueMod.ini                      your NPC list and physiques[/code]

That is the whole mod. Zip it from [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]MyPhysiqueMod\[/font][/size] and it installs like any other - no ESP needed, no FaceGen, no scripts.

NOTES:

[list]
[*][size=2][b]The .tri filename is yours to choose,[/b] nothing is inferred from it. But every mod shares this one folder, so prefix it to avoid conflicts![/size][/*]
[*][size=2][b]The manifest is yours too.[/b] MPF reads every .toml in that folder, so name it after your mod and it never replaces anybody else's.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2][b]The INI is yours alone.[/b] MPF reads every .ini in that folder, recursively, and resolves conflicts by Priority - you never edit another mod's file.[/size][/*]
[/list]

[b]You never declare which head mod you authored for[/b]

There is no "topology" field anywhere. Sculpt the morph on the head you want to
support, save the .tri from that mesh, and the plugin works the rest out:

[list=1]
[*][size=2][b]Vertex count[/b] - read from your .tri, not declared. This alone separates COtR,
High Poly COtR, High Poly Head, Alternate High Poly Head and vanilla.[/size][/*]
[*][size=2][b]Shape[/b] - the live mesh is compared against your .tri's rest pose, with the mean
position removed so it compares form, not world position. Too far apart and your
set is refused for that head.[/size][/*]
[/list]

Want to cover two head mods? Ship two .tri files, one sculpted on each. A head
nobody authored for is left alone - it does not break, it just does not change.

[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- INSTALLATION[/u][/b][/size][/color]

Install MPF once, then install any content mod that supports it. Do not merge or rename content INIs or manifests from different mods. MPF loads every INI under [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]Data\ModernPhysiqueFramework\[/font][/size] and every .toml under [size=2][font=Monaco, Menlo, Consolas, monospace]Data\SKSE\Plugins\MPF\morphs\[/font][/size], and resolves conflicts deterministically.


[color=#e69138][size=5][b][u]- COMPATIBILITY[/u][/b][/size][/color]

MPF does not replace FaceGen, edit NPC records permanently, or require a specific NPC replacer.
Outfit distribution and body assets belong to the content mod.
